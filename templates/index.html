Welcome to Nexora.AI

Nexora.AI is an advanced document intelligence and conversational RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) platform. It allows teams to upload corporate files (PDFs, text files, documents) and explore them through unified semantic search, natural language QA, and a suite of visual analytical tools.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

  1. Upload Your Documents: Click the "Upload Files" area in the sidebar to upload PDFs or text documents. Once uploaded, the files are parsed and indexed semantically.
  2. Ask Questions (RAG QA): Use the primary chat companion interface to type questions. Nexora will search across all uploaded files, fetch the most relevant paragraphs, and generate an answer citing the sources.
  3. Inspect Citations: Click the highlighted source badge on any response to open the "Source Context Inspector" and read the exact source paragraphs.
  4. Explore Analytics Features: Click the "Explore Features" dropdown at the top to access specialized tools like LexiScope, Mind Map, Knowledge Graph, Cluster Universe, and Action Item Tracker.

Explore Features in Detail

Intelligence
Executive Report Compiles and generates a comprehensive, AI-written PDF or DOCX report summarising all findings, insights, and key metrics from the selected set of documents.
Action Item & Decision Tracker Scans documents using AI to automatically identify hidden action items, deadlines, owners, and decisions, organizing them inside a fully operational Kanban board.
TrendLens Compares your documents against live web intelligence—news, tweets, reviews, research, and trends—providing a "Doc vs. World" verification report that can be exported as a PDF.
Knowledge Discovery
Knowledge Graph Visualizes connections between concepts, entities, and source documents in a dynamic force-directed network. Helps pinpoint key relationships across separate files.
Cluster Universe Embeds your files into a 2D coordinate space based on semantic meaning. Nearby points represent documents with similar context, giving you a bird's-eye view of your entire dataset.
TimelineWeave Plots your document's topics chronologically against real-world events sourced live from the web, weaving dates, news, and research into a structured timeline report.
Visual Analytics
Visual Pulse Turns your document's core concepts and topics into a live, visual mood-board gallery sourced fresh from the web.
StatSonar Displays live statistical big-number cards, cross-source sparkline charts, and a source-credibility heatmap, counting and grouping references directly.
Advanced Analytics
LexiScope Suite Analyzes text to extract overall emotional sentiment, create word clouds of frequent keywords, discover main topics, identify keyphrases, extract named entities (people/places/dates), and calculate readability scores.

System FAQ

Q: What is TrendLens and how does it work? A: TrendLens extracts key claims and topics from your uploaded documents and runs real-time web searches to check them against live internet resources. It compiles this evidence to show whether your documents are confirmed, contradicted, or outdated relative to current information.
Q: What is TimelineWeave? A: TimelineWeave automatically identifies the primary themes in your documents and graphs them chronologically alongside live web events, tweets, news, and milestones. It helps you see how your document's storyline aligns with real-world history.
Q: What is Visual Pulse? A: Visual Pulse generates an interactive image gallery and visual mood-board. It analyzes document topics and pairs them with relevant real-world images searched live from the web, providing visual context to text-based reports.
Q: What is StatSonar? A: StatSonar displays live metric counts, animated gauge cards, and source-credibility grids. It counts web references across news, blogs, and social feeds for your topics and groups them into credibility tiers to analyze source patterns.
Q: How does the AI search across my files? A: The system splits documents into paragraphs, embeds them into a semantic vector database, and matches your questions using dense cosine similarity to locate the most relevant sections before generating answers.
Q: Can I change colors or look? A: Yes! Click the gear icon in the top right to open "Session Settings". You can select from 6 themes (Lavender, Cyan, Sunset, Sherbet, Obsidian, Tropical) and toggle between dark and light appearance modes.
Q: How do I export my conversation? A: Use the floating "Export PDF" button in the chat area to download a beautifully formatted transcript of your session.

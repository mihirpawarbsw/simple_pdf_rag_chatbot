How it works

Three steps. No learning curve.

No setup, no manuals, no technical know-how needed. If you can attach a file to an email, you already know how to use Nexora.

STEP 2

Select your analysis tool

Converse directly with your documents, construct visual connection graphs, track deadlines and tasks, or run deep semantic theme clustering across the workspace.

STEP 3

Extract intelligence

Get source-cited answers, download structured executive reports, or inspect visual mind maps with absolute transparency, validation, and clarity.

Explore every tool

One workspace, seven superpowers.

Behind the simple chat box sits a full toolkit. Open any of these any time — no extra setup, just a click.

Knowledge Graph

See how everything in your documents connects — people, companies, topics, and ideas, drawn as a simple web.

  • Spot relationships you'd otherwise miss
  • Click any point to jump to the source
  • Works across all your files at once

Interactive Mind Map

Turns a long document into a visual tree of its main ideas, so you can see the big picture in seconds.

  • Built automatically — nothing to draw yourself
  • Expand any branch for more detail
  • Great for studying or skimming reports

Cluster Universe

Automatically sorts a big pile of files into neat topic groups, so related documents find each other.

  • No folders or tags to manage by hand
  • Each group comes with a short summary
  • Perfect for messy, unsorted archives

Document Analytics

Get an instant read on tone, key phrases, and how complex a document is — without reading it cover to cover.

  • Tells you if the tone is positive or negative
  • Highlights the most-used ideas and terms
  • Flags whether something is a hard read

Smart Action Tracker

Reads meeting notes and reports for you, then pulls out every task, deadline, and decision automatically.

  • Nothing falls through the cracks
  • Drag tasks across a simple to-do board
  • See who's responsible for what

One-Click Report Builder

Turns a whole folder of documents into one clean, readable report — ready to download or share.

  • Includes a summary and key takeaways
  • Export straight to PDF or Word
  • Saves hours of manual copy-pasting

The Engine Behind It All

Every tool above runs on the same accuracy-first engine: it searches your real documents before it ever answers.

  • Answers are checked against your files first
  • Built-in guardrails reduce made-up answers

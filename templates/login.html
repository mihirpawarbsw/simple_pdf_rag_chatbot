01 — Core

Hybrid AI Search

Combines dense vector retrieval and BM25 sparse retrieval for highly accurate semantic and keyword-aware document search. The fusion engine merges semantic similarity with keyword precision, ensuring no relevant passage is missed across large enterprise corpora.

02

Multiuser RAG Workspace

Secure isolated vector databases and personalized AI workspaces for each user and organization. Documents, embeddings, and conversation history are fully separated at the database level with no cross-user data leakage.

03

AI Guardrails & Security

Built-in prompt injection protection, jailbreak detection, PII identification, and hallucination reduction. Enterprise-safe RAG with grounded, explainable, source-verified answers.

04

Semantic PDF Intelligence

Upload PDFs and instantly ask contextual questions, generate summaries, extract insights, and discover knowledge hidden across hundreds of pages in seconds.

05

Streaming AI Conversations

Real-time AI response streaming with contextual multi-turn memory and source-grounded answers. Responsive conversational interactions grounded to your documents.

06

Source Verification

Every answer is backed by verified document chunks and page-level citations for explainable AI responses. Complete traceability for compliance, auditing, and trust.

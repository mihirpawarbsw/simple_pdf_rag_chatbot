FAQ

Common questions

Everything you need to know about how Nexora.AI works.

Nexora.AI supports PDF, DOCX (Word), TXT, and CSV files. Support covers text-heavy documents, structured reports, data files, and formatted business documents. Each file is parsed, chunked, and embedded automatically upon upload.

When you upload a document, Nexora.AI extracts the text, segments it into meaningful chunks, and generates dense semantic embeddings stored in ChromaDB. It also builds a BM25 sparse index for keyword retrieval. Both indexes are queried together using a hybrid fusion strategy to deliver the most relevant results.

Absolutely not. Each user's documents, vector embeddings, and conversation history are stored in fully isolated database namespaces. There is no cross-user data access. Your knowledge base is only accessible to your account.

Nexora.AI supports multilingual documents and queries. You can upload documents in multiple languages, ask questions in your preferred language, and receive responses in that same language. Cross-lingual retrieval is also supported — query in English against a French document, for example.

Yes. You can set custom system instructions per knowledge base to tune the AI's response style, persona, and focus. You can also select preferred AI models and configure response preferences such as verbosity and citation format.

Nexora.AI includes prompt injection protection, jailbreak detection, PII identification in responses, and hallucination reduction through retrieval grounding. The system is designed to stay focused on your document content and refuse attempts to redirect it outside the knowledge scope.

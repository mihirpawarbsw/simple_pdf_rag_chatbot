How it works

Four steps. No learning curve.

No complex configurations or training manuals. If you can click a button, you already know how to use Nexora.AI.

STEP 1

Ingest & Index Files

Upload PDFs, Word documents, spreadsheets, or plain text. Once uploaded, Nexora automatically parses and indexes them semantically.

STEP 2

Ask Questions (RAG)

Ask in natural language. Nexora semantic search fetches relevant paragraphs and drafts answers directly citing original sources.

STEP 3

Inspect Citations

Click the highlighted source badges on any answer to open the Inspector and read the exact source document passages in real time.

STEP 4

Explore Analytics

Access specialized tools like sentiment analytics, mind maps, knowledge graphs, semantic cluster universes, and action trackers.

What you get

Everything you need, nothing you don't.

A quick look at the highlights — then a full walkthrough of every tool just below.

Deep Context Comprehension

Nexora analyzes semantic meanings and sentence relationships to understand the core message of your files, far beyond keyword matching.

Zero Hallucination Guarantee

Answers are strictly bounded to your uploaded files to prevent fabrications. If a claim isn't in your corpus, the model won't invent it.

Automated Metadata Tagging

Automatically identifies and extracts key named entities (people, dates, places) and specialized terminology across your documents.

Cross-Document Synthesis

Ask questions that span multiple separate documents. Nexora correlates information across PDF, DOCX, and TXT files simultaneously.

Custom Workspace Styling

Personalize your session with 5 premium visual themes, adjustable response lengths, and light/dark modes tailored for your workflow.

Private & Secure Sandbox

Your files, database indexes, and chat histories are isolated in your account. Zero model training and complete data privacy.

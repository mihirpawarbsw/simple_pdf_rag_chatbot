How it works

Three steps. No learning curve.

No setup, no manuals, no technical know-how needed. If you can attach a file to an email, you already know how to use Nexora.

STEP 1

Add your documents

Drop in PDFs, Word files, spreadsheets, or plain text. Nexora reads and organizes them automatically — nothing to configure.

STEP 2

Ask a question

Type your question like you're texting a knowledgeable friend. "What's our refund policy?" "Summarize this contract."

STEP 3

Get a trusted answer

Nexora replies in plain language and shows you exactly which document and page it came from — so you can always double-check.

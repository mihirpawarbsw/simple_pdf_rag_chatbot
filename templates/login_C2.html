01 — Core

Hybrid Retrieval Architecture

Nexora.AI combines dense vector retrieval with BM25 sparse search for unparalleled document recall. The fusion engine merges semantic similarity with keyword precision, ensuring no relevant passage is missed — even across large enterprise corpora with thousands of document chunks.

02

Multiuser RAG Workspace

Each user operates within a securely isolated vector namespace. Documents, embeddings, and conversation history are fully separated at the database level — no cross-user data leakage, ever.

03

AI Guardrails & Security

Built-in prompt injection defense, jailbreak detection, PII identification, and hallucination reduction. Enterprise-safe RAG with grounded, explainable, source-verified answers.

04

Semantic PDF Intelligence

Upload PDFs and instantly query them in natural language. Get contextual summaries, extract key insights, and discover knowledge hidden across hundreds of pages — in seconds.

05

Streaming AI Conversations

Real-time token streaming for responsive, conversational interactions. Multi-turn memory with contextual continuity across the full session, all grounded to your documents.

06

Source Verification

Every AI response cites the exact document chunks it was derived from. No fabricated references. Complete traceability for compliance, auditing, and trust.

02
How it works

Four steps. No configuration.

There's no schema to define or pipeline to wire up. Drop in files, and everything downstream — indexing, retrieval, analysis — happens automatically.

Upload & index

Drop in PDFs, Word docs, PowerPoints, or text files. Nexora.AI splits them into paragraphs and embeds each one into a semantic vector index.

Ask in plain language

Type or speak a question. Dense retrieval matches it against your documents and an LLM answers, grounded only in what it finds.

Check the source

Every answer carries a citation badge. Click it to open the Source Inspector and read the exact paragraph and page it came from.

Go deeper

Open the analysis suite for knowledge graphs, action-item extraction, sentiment and readability scoring, or a live web fact-check.

03
The architecture

A retrieval layer, not a black box.

Nexora.AI doesn't send your documents into a model's memory and hope for the best. It retrieves the exact passages relevant to your question, then asks the model to answer from those passages alone.

You ask
A question, in plain language or by voice
Nexora.AI retrieves & reasons
Vector search finds the relevant passages · your chosen LLM answers from them · every claim keeps its citation
Your document library
PDFs, Word files, slide decks, and plain text — indexed paragraph by paragraph

05
Explore every tool

One chat box, nine analysis engines.

Grouped into four categories inside the workspace — Intelligence, Knowledge Discovery, Visual Analytics, and Advanced Analytics — each tool runs on the documents you've already uploaded. No extra setup.

Knowledge Graph

Visualizes people, entities, and topics as a force-directed network, so relationships across separate files become visible at a glance.

  • Surfaces connections you'd miss reading linearly
  • Click any node to jump to its source
  • Spans every document in the workspace

Cluster Universe

Embeds every file into a 2D semantic space — documents that sit close together are close in meaning, giving you a bird's-eye map of the whole library.

  • No manual tagging required
  • Reveals overlap in messy archives
  • Built on the same embeddings as search

LexiScope Suite

Runs full NLP on your text: sentiment, keyphrases, named entities, topics, a word cloud, and a readability score.

  • Overall emotional tone
  • People, places, and dates extracted
  • Reading-difficulty scoring

Action Tracker

Scans documents for hidden action items, deadlines, owners, and decisions, then organizes them on a working Kanban board.

  • Nothing buried in a report gets missed
  • Kanban or timeline view
  • Filter by type, owner, or status

Executive Report

Compiles an AI-written summary of every finding, insight, and key metric across your selected documents into one report.

  • Synthesizes across the whole file set
  • Exports as PDF or DOCX
  • Ready to forward as-is

TrendLens

Pulls key claims from your documents and checks them against live web search — news, research, reviews — to see what still holds up.

  • Confirmed, contradicted, or outdated — flagged clearly
  • Sources cited from the live web
  • Exports as a "Doc vs. World" PDF

TimelineWeave

Identifies the primary themes in your documents and plots them chronologically next to real-world news and events.

  • See how your document's story lines up with history
  • Pulled live from the web
  • Exports as a structured timeline report

StatSonar

Surfaces live big-number stat cards and cross-source sparklines, grouping web references by source credibility.

  • Counts references across news, blogs, and social
  • Source-credibility heatmap
  • Built for a fast gut-check on a topic

Visual Pulse

Turns your document's core topics into a live image gallery, pairing text-heavy reports with real visual context.

  • Images sourced fresh from the web
  • Built for briefs and presentations
  • One click from any topic
